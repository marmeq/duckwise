Privacy Policy

Duckwise: Duck Hunting Weather · Last updated November 12, 2026

Duckwise forecasts hunting conditions and keeps an optional log of your hunts. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Duckwise has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not run a server and do not receive any of your data.

Location

If you allow it, your iPhone's approximate location is used to get the weather where you are. Weather comes from Apple Weather (WeatherKit): the app sends a location to Apple to get a forecast, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see or store it. You can deny location and use saved spots instead.

Your spots and hunts

Spot names and positions, blind directions, birds, notes and the weather saved with each hunt are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting a spot, a hunt or the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Children

Duckwise is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, feature ideas or a forecast that missed? Open an issue at github.com/marmeq/duckwise/issues.

How the hunt score works

  • Every morning starts at 50. Cold fronts, rising pressure behind them, steady wind of roughly 10–20 mph, grey skies, rain or snow, freezing nights and new birds pushed down from the north raise it.
  • Calm bluebird days, warm-ups, heat, gales and a full moon (birds feed at night) lower it.
  • Migration push watch looks at the weather 200 and 400 miles north of you: a hard freeze or snow there tends to bring new birds within a day or two.
  • Blind wind check: ducks land into the wind, so a wind at your back brings them over the decoys facing you.

Duckwise is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Always check your state's seasons, bag limits and legal shooting hours, and never go out on the water in unsafe wind or ice. Weather data provided by Apple Weather.
